# Terms of service (source of truth for /terms)

1. Provided **as-is**, no warranty, not externally audited.
2. Owners are solely responsible for uploaded content; the operator is cryptographically blind
   and cannot moderate content proactively.
3. Acceptable use: no malware, illegal material, infringement, rate-limit evasion or
   share-code enumeration.
4. **No DRM claims** — watermark/download-off/present-mode are deterrents only.
5. Free-tier availability: caps, cold starts and DB auto-suspend may affect uptime. No SLA.
6. Deletion crypto-shreds ciphertext; with no key on the server the content becomes unrecoverable.
7. Losing both the `#k=` fragment and the password makes a document permanently unrecoverable.
8. Changes are tracked in CHANGELOG.md.
